Adaptive Engram Memory System for Indonesian Language ModelGenerative AI Based on TOBA LM for Batak and Minang Language

arXiv ↗ SSRN ↗ Hugging Face ↗

Language is the starting point. TOBA-LM explores modeling for Indonesian, Batak, and Minangkabau, with a focus on low-resource languages.

The approach brings together syllabic-agglutinative tokenization and adaptive Engram Memory based on n-grams.

The 1.2B-parameter model reached convergence in 12,973 steps. The paper reports approximately 80% fewer training steps than its conventional-transformer comparison of over 70,000 steps.
