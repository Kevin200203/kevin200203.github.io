Adaptive Engram Memory System for Indonesian Language Model:Generative AI Based on TOBA LM for Batak and Minang Language

Hokky Situngkir, Kevin Siringoringo, Andhika Bernard Lumbantobing · arXiv, 2026

arXiv ↗ SSRN ↗ Hugging Face ↗

Millions of people speak Batak and Minangkabau, yet high-quality language-model data remain scarce. The paper cites Indonesia’s statistics agency, BPS (2011): approximately 8.47 million Batak speakers and 4.2 million Minang speakers.

The approach brings together syllabic-agglutinative tokenization and adaptive Engram Memory based on n-grams.

The 1.2B-parameter model reached convergence in 12,973 steps. The paper reports approximately 80% fewer training steps than its conventional-transformer comparison of over 70,000 steps.
